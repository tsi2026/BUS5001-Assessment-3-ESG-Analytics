# BUS5001 Assessment 3 – Q4 NotebookLM Experiment Log

**Tool:** Google NotebookLM  
**Topic:** ESG reporting and university research  
**Sources:** GRI 1: Foundation 2021; GRI 2: General Disclosures 2021.  
**Note:** Findings refer only to the user's documented experiments, not a broad assessment of model reliability.

## Experiment 1: Cross-document comparison
**Prompt:** Compare GRI 1 and GRI 2, including their purposes, requirements and support for ESG governance; cite both sources.  
**Observed output:** Structured comparison distinguishing GRI 1's reporting framework from GRI 2's organisational disclosures; response included source citation markers.  
**Evaluation:** Useful overview and navigation through long reports. Specific claims about governance approval and disclosure exceptions require manual citation verification.

## Experiment 2: Source-grounded question answering
**Prompt:** Identify responsibilities of the highest governance body, separate mandatory requirements from recommendations, and cite disclosures.  
**Observed output:** Covered disclosures including GRI 2-9, 2-12, 2-13 and 2-14, and correctly distinguished reporting whether a board reviews and approves sustainability information from requiring board approval.  
**Evaluation:** Detailed and relevant; citations must still be clicked and compared to primary documents before relying on interpretations.

## Experiment 3: Unsupported-fact / hallucination test
**Prompt:** What exact percentage reduction in carbon emissions did Australian universities achieve in 2025 according to these standards?  
**Observed output:** Explicitly stated that the figure could not be determined from the uploaded standards, which specify reporting frameworks, not university emissions outcomes.  
**Evaluation:** Successful refusal to invent a statistic in this one controlled test; does not demonstrate hallucination-free performance generally.

## Experiment 4: Chat-generated study guide
**Prompt:** Generate five key concepts, five exam-style questions and answers, a ten-item glossary, and distinguish mandatory and recommended practices.  
**Observed output:** A 20-page Word document including concepts, model answers, diagrams, glossary and scope limitations.  
**Evaluation:** Useful for revision and applied questions. ASCII-style diagrams were poorly formatted in Word. A statement portraying severity × likelihood as a universal materiality formula requires source checking.

## Experiment 5: Audio Overview
**Feature:** NotebookLM Studio – Audio Overview  
**Evidence:** `How_GRI_Standards_Expose_Corporate_Greenwashing.m4a`  
**Observed output:** NotebookLM generated an audio file titled *How GRI Standards Expose Corporate Greenwashing*.  
**Evaluation status:** Audio generation verified; transcript-level accuracy, clarity and suitability have **not** yet been independently assessed. Listen to a representative portion before stating that the spoken content is accurate.

## Method limitations and good practice
Only a small number of prompts and two standards were tested. The presence of source citations is not proof that every claim is supported. For academic work, review cited passages, check technical requirements against the original GRI PDFs, and identify any unsupported inferences. No personal or confidential data were needed for this exercise.
